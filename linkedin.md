 🚀 **Motisha is now live in production**

I’m excited to share a platform I’ve been building — **Motisha**, a digital learning and professional development platform designed specifically for Kenyan teachers.

Teachers play a huge role in shaping the future, yet access to quality, structured, and affordable professional development content is still limited.

Motisha aims to change that.

### 📚 What teachers can do on Motisha

* Access a curated library of speeches, newsletters, and learning materials aligned to the Kenyan CBC calendar
* Enroll in online courses with progress tracking
* Book motivational and leadership services
* Refer colleagues and earn commissions
* Manage subscriptions (trial, Pro, and School plans)

We also include a **7-day free trial** so teachers can explore before committing.

### 🛠️ What’s behind the platform

Built as a full-stack system using **Next.js 14 + Supabase**, Motisha includes:

* Secure role-based access (teachers & admins)
* M-Pesa STK Push integration for payments
* Automated subscriptions, referrals, and notifications via Postgres logic
* Real-time updates and analytics for admins
* Custom-built admin dashboard with full content and user management
